# Workflow & Project Standards

## 1. Revenue & Marketing Alignment
- Every workflow, pitch, and content asset must tie back to driving measurable business revenue and reducing manual friction.

## 2. PMM Positioning Standards
- Maintain authentic credibility: highlight real skills in workflow automation, AI agents, project management, and product marketing without overstating past roles.

## 3. Client Delivery & Approvals
- Draft copy and media assets with clear client sign-off prompts.
- Maintain standardized intake forms and automated reminder sequences (e.g., birthday campaigns, onboarding).
