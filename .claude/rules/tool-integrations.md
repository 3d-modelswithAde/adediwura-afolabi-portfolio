# Tool Integrations & Execution Rules

## Connected Integrations
- **n8n MCP Server:** Use for creating, inspecting, testing, and managing automated workflows, triggers, and webhook integrations.
- **Google Workspace (`gws` CLI):** Use for Google Sheets databases, Drive file management, Calendar scheduling, Docs, and Gmail workflows.

## Client Asset & Channel Management
- **Visuals & Media:** Prepare Canva assets and copy for client approval prior to publication.
- **Channels:** Format messages appropriately for Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram, and LinkedIn.
- **CRM:** Keep HubSpot contact properties and deal stages accurate when managing client leads.
