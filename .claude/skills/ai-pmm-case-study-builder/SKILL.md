# AI PMM Case Study Builder

This skill translates raw technical projects (such as Marcus Vance Automotive CRM intake, GText Homes Voice Sales, and The Visionary Nation Birthday Automation) into recruiter-ready Product Marketing Case Studies.

---

## 1. The PMM Case Study Framework (STAR-I)

Every case study follows the elite PMM narrative structure:

1. **The Situation & Customer Problem:**
   - Who was the user/stakeholder?
   - What was broken in the manual status quo? (Time wasted, lost revenue, user anxiety).
2. **The Strategic Positioning & Hypothesis:**
   - Why automation was the right product solution.
   - How the system was positioned to end-users (e.g. "Human-in-the-loop assistant" vs. "Black-box AI").
3. **The Product Architecture & Execution:**
   - The core mechanics (n8n, Google Forms, Sheets, Gmail, deterministic safeguards).
   - How edge cases and user trust were solved (e.g. removing direct WhatsApp risk).
4. **The Measurable Impact (The "I"):**
   - Quantified metrics: % time saved, 0% error rate, 100% on-time delivery.
5. **Key PMM Learnings & Iterations:**
   - User feedback loops and how customer sentiment shaped product pivots.
