## 1. Core Frameworks & Invariants

### A. The 5-Point Positioning Statement Formula
Every AI product or service must answer:
```
For [Target Audience / ICP]
Who [Painful Unmet Need or Friction],
[Product / Service Name] is an [AI Product Category / Vehicle]
That [Primary Value Proposition & Transformation].
Unlike [Existing Alternatives / Manual Status Quo],
Our System [Key Differentiator / Proprietary Unfair Advantage].
```

### B. The 3-Tier Messaging Hierarchy
When drafting copy for landing pages, sales briefs, or pitch decks:
- **Tier 1: Strategic Hook (Headline / Value Proposition)**
  - Focus on business outcome (Revenue generated, hours saved, error rates eliminated).
  - Avoid feature-centric jargon ("Powered by LLM") in favor of capability ("Autonomous 24/7 lead qualification").
- **Tier 2: Core Value Pillars (3 Mutually Exclusive Pillars)**
  - **Speed / Efficiency**: Instant response, zero latency.
  - **Reliability / Accuracy**: Zero-hallucination deterministic business logic.
  - **Scalability / Cost**: Reduces manual labor while 10x-ing throughput.
- **Tier 3: Feature-to-Benefit Proof Points**
  - Feature: *Native CRM & Google Sheets Sync*
  - Benefit: *Zero lost leads and automated real-time advisor alerts.*

## 2. Product Marketing Deliverables Generated

### A. Positioning & Messaging Matrix (PMM Brief)
Generates:
1. **Target Persona (ICP):** Roles, pain points, daily workflow friction, metrics they are judged on.
2. **Value Drivers:** 3 distinct value pillars tailored to decision-makers (e.g. CMO, Head of Sales, Operations Director).
3. **Elevator Pitch:** 30-second conversational summary.
4. **Objection Handling Matrix:** Anticipated client/buyer pushbacks and deterministic rebuttals.

### B. Product Launch Plan (GTM Checklist)
Generates an operational roadmap:
- **Pre-Launch:** Beta validation, lighthouse customer testimonials, asset creation.
- **Launch Day:** Multi-channel distribution (LinkedIn thought leadership, email newsletter, direct outreach).
- **Post-Launch:** Tracking adoption KPIs, gathering customer feedback loops, iterative feature marketing.
