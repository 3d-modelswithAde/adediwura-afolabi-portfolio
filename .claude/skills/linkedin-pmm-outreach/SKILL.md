# LinkedIn PMM Outreach & Mentorship Engine

## Overview
Generate high-converting, authentic LinkedIn connection requests, direct messages, and relationship-building sequences targeted at **Senior Product Marketing Managers, Mentors, and PMM Community Leaders** to build genuine relationships and unlock referral paths for Junior/Internship AI PMM roles.

---

## 🎯 Target Persona & Context
- **Primary Audience:** Senior PMMs, Lead PMMs, Group Product Marketing Managers, and Mentors (African tech startups, growth-stage companies, and remote global tech).
- **Core Goal:** Build authentic mentorship relationships, seek expert advice on real work, and position Adediwura for junior/internship PMM opportunities.
- **Narrative Bridge:** Mechanical Engineering Design (systems thinking) + Social Media Management (audience storytelling) + AI/Workflow Automation (revenue systems) ➔ **AI Product Marketing Manager**.

---

## ✍️ Communication & Voice Rules
1. **Conversational & Natural:** Write like a real person having a one-on-one dialogue over coffee. No stiff corporate jargon.
2. **Emotional Resonance & Empathy:** Acknowledge their craft, real career milestones, or insights from their posts.
3. **Respectful & Zero Entitlement:** Never demand a job or call outright. Lead with curiosity, appreciation, and specific questions.
4. **Brevity & Clarity:** Keep connection notes strictly under 300 characters. Keep DMs under 120 words with plenty of whitespace.

---

## 📋 Standard Workflow

When the user asks to draft outreach for a person/profile:

### Step 1: Profile & Context Extraction
Identify:
- **Target Name:** e.g., Sarah
- **Target Title & Company:** e.g., Lead PMM at Paystack / Flutterwave / Moniepoint
- **Hook / Shared Point:** Recent LinkedIn post, shared community (e.g., PMM Alliance, Non-Tech in Tech), or specific career transition admiration.
- **Stage of Outreach:** Connection request, initial DM, feedback request, or follow-up.

### Step 2: Select the Message Framework

#### Framework 1: The 300-Character Connection Note (Cold Connect)
*Goal: 70%+ acceptance rate by demonstrating genuine interest.*
```text
Hi [Name], loved your recent insight on [specific topic/post]. As someone transitioning from engineering & SMM into AI PMM, I really admire your journey at [Company]. Would love to connect and follow your work!
```

#### Framework 2: The "15-Minute Advice / Story" DM (Post-Connection)
*Goal: Low-friction request for career advice or insights.*
```text
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

I’ve been following how you approach [PMM area, e.g., product messaging / GTM] at [Company]—the clarity in your work stands out.

I’m currently navigating the transition from engineering design & social media into AI Product Marketing, focusing on building automated revenue workflows for products. 

If your schedule permits sometime this month, I would be grateful for a quick 15-minute virtual chat to hear 1–2 lessons from your early PMM days. No pressure at all if you’re booked up—cheering on your work either way!
```

#### Framework 3: The "Work / Tear-down Feedback" DM
*Goal: Prove competency by sharing a tangible piece of work and asking for 2 minutes of critique.*
```text
Hi [Name], hope your week is going well!

I recently put together a breakdown / AI workflow case study on [Topic: e.g., Real Estate Voice Agent positioning / GTM framework for local businesses]. 

Given your deep experience in [their domain], I’d love to get 2 minutes of your critical feedback on where I can sharpen the positioning: [Link/Brief Summary].

Would love to hear your raw thoughts if you have a moment!
```

#### Framework 4: The Value-Add Follow-Up
*Goal: Close the loop after receiving advice or engaging on their posts.*
```text
Hi [Name], just wanted to drop a quick note: I applied the advice you shared regarding [Topic] to a recent project, and [specific positive outcome/learning]. 

Really appreciate your guidance—it made a noticeable difference!
```

---


#### Framework 5: The "SMM Proof + Volunteer Contribution" DM (High-Leverage Value Add)
*Goal: Showcase tangible social media execution wins and offer to take work off their plate on active projects/launches.*
```text
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting!

I’ve been following how you lead product marketing at [Company]—the narrative clarity in your recent campaigns is incredible.

On my end, my foundation is in social media management and growth marketing, where I’ve managed end-to-end campaigns—from drafting high-converting copy and visual assets to running automated content distribution workflows. Right now, I’m channeling that audience empathy and execution speed into AI Product Marketing.

I know how demanding GTM launches and content repurposing can be. If you have any side projects, case studies, or distribution tasks on your desk that you need an extra pair of hands on, I’d love to volunteer my time to help you build or execute them—no strings attached.

Would love to support what you’re building if an opportunity aligns!
```

## ⚡ Output Requirements for Assistant
Whenever drafting outreach:
1. Provide **Option A (Story & Advice Focus)** and **Option B (Work & Feedback Focus)**.
2. Display character count for connection notes to ensure they fit under LinkedIn's 300-character limit.
3. Include clear `[Placeholders]` where specific details (post topics, company names) can be customized in 5 seconds.
