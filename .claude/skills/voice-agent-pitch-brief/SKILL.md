# Voice Agent Pitch Brief

This skill equips the agent to craft consultative, enterprise-grade pitch decks, sales 1-pagers, and ROI business cases for deploying autonomous AI Voice Agents (powered by ElevenLabs, Twilio, n8n, and CRM integrations).

---

## 1. The GText Homes / Real Estate Value Proposition

### A. The Core Problem Solved
- High-ticket real estate leads go cold when response time exceeds 5 minutes.
- Manual sales agents waste 70% of their day calling invalid numbers or unqualified leads.
- Inbound inquiries from international buyers (diaspora) arrive outside Nigerian working hours.

### B. The AI Voice Solution
- **Instant Outreach:** Voice agent initiates human-like consultation call within 60 seconds of form submission.
- **Strict Verification:** Validates Nigerian phone numbers (+234) and scrubs invalid formats before routing.
- **Smart Matching:** Pairs customer location/budget with verified luxury estates (e.g. Jasper Smart City Abuja, Jasper Gardenia Lagos).
- **Human-in-the-Loop Safety:** Sensitive queries (refunds, legal, complaints) are immediately quarantined and routed to human directors.

---

## 2. Standard Pitch Deliverable Structure

When invoked, this skill outputs a 4-part executive brief:

1. **Executive Summary:** The business opportunity and quantified cost of lead decay.
2. **System Architecture & Safety:** How the workflow guarantees zero hallucinations and human supervision.
3. **Financial ROI Model:**
   - Conversion increase from 5-minute response vs. 24-hour response.
   - Cost per lead qualified vs. full-time human cold-callers.
4. **Implementation Roadmap:**
